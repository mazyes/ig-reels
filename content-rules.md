# Content rules: AI news for business owners

Read this before writing any reel. It is the brief for every post.

## Who we talk to
Owners of small and mid-size businesses (clinics, salons, restaurants, agencies, shops, real estate, local services) in the US, UK, EU and Gulf. Not AI fans, not developers. They are busy and they want to know: "does this change how I make money?"

## The one rule
Every reel = one AI news story + **what it means for your business** + **one thing to do this week**.
Never report news without the business angle. If a story has no business angle, skip it.

## Story picking (score 1-10, take the highest)
- Affects how customers find, book, or pay a business (search, maps, WhatsApp, Instagram, booking, payments): +4
- A tool an owner can actually use this week: +3
- Big brand (Google, Meta, OpenAI, Apple, Microsoft, Anthropic): +2
- Purely technical (benchmarks, model sizes, funding rounds, research papers): skip

## Reel shape (20-35 seconds, 5-7 slides)
1. HOOK: under 12 words, says what changed and who it hits. A number or a brand name in it. No greetings, no "stop scrolling", no questions you can answer with "no".
2. WHAT HAPPENED: one slide, plain words.
3. WHY IT MATTERS FOR YOU: 1-2 slides, concrete (lost customers, saved hours, money).
4. DO THIS: one action they can take this week.
5. CTA: "Comment AI and I'll send you the checklist" or "Follow for one AI move a day."

## Voice
- Short sentences. Spoken English, grade-6 reading level.
- Confident, a little blunt, never hype. No "game-changer", "revolutionary", "unlock", "dive in", "in today's fast-paced world".
- No em dashes. No emojis in slides. Max 1 emoji in the caption.
- Numbers as digits ("3x", "$40").
- Never invent facts. Every claim must come from the source article. If unsure, leave it out.

## Visual style (cinematic, minimal)
- Dark, desaturated b-roll behind every slide: cities at night, laptops, offices, phones, empty stores. Pick a `broll` search term per slide that matches the line.
- Captions 3 words at a time, uppercase, white. Put 1-3 key words per slide in `highlight` (they turn gold). Numbers turn gold automatically.
- Calm, slow, confident narration. No hype words, no exclamation marks.
- Keep the whole reel to 20-35 seconds: about 70-90 spoken words total at this voice speed.

## Caption
- First 125 characters repeat the hook's payoff, not the hook itself.
- 3-5 lines of body, one CTA, source link name ("Source: The Verge").
- Max 5 hashtags: #ainews #smallbusiness plus 2-3 for the story.

## Posting
- 1 reel per day. Never the same story twice (check `posted.json`).
- Default time: 13:00 UTC (8-9am US East, 2pm Europe, 5pm Dubai). Move it once we have 14 days of Insights.
